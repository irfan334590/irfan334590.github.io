01 /

Biomedical AI with a clear translational direction: from data to care.

Academic identity

I am a Senior Researcher, Principal Investigator, Visiting Research Fellow and Supervisor working in artificial intelligence for healthcare. My expertise spans health monitoring, prevention, intervention and treatment, wearable physiological sensing, edge AI, federated learning and translational medical imaging.

I completed my first PhD in Biomedical Engineering at Fudan University in December 2024 and my second PhD in Information and Communication Engineering at the University of Turku in April 2026. Together, these degrees connect biomedical measurement, signal processing, imaging and computationally efficient AI.

What I focus on

I focus on translating multimodal sleep, cardiovascular, physiological and imaging biomarkers into deployable AI systems for dementia care, CVD risk prediction and personalised monitoring.

Research visualisation

My research follows a simple path: collect meaningful biomedical data, build efficient and privacy-aware AI models, and translate the outputs into dementia care, CVD risk prediction and treatment planning.

Biomedical data

I start from multimodal signals, images and clinical variables: ECG, PPG, EEG, MRI, CT, PET and wearable streams.

Efficient AI

I build lightweight, explainable and privacy-aware models using CNNs, ensembles, transfer learning and federated learning.

Edge and federation

The models are designed for edge–cloud deployment and multi-site learning where sensitive data should not move unnecessarily.

Clinical translation

The output is risk prediction, monitoring, treatment support and dashboards for dementia care and cardiovascular risk.
