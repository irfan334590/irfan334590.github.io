01 / About

A clinician-adjacent AI researcher with two doctorates and a translational instinct.

Research focus

My work integrates AI-driven cardiovascular and dementia risk prediction, wearable physiological sensing, and translational cardiac and neuroimaging — with the overarching aim of quantifying and addressing the heart–brain axis. I design LiteML architectures: computationally efficient CNNs and hybrid models built for deployment on edge–cloud infrastructures and integration into clinical and home-care pathways.

Current foundations include polysomnography-derived foundation models translated to wrist-worn devices (Polar 360), federated learning for dementia-care wearables, and digital twins for chemotherapy toxicity reduction.

03 / Research

Five threads, one through-line: efficient AI that survives contact with patients.

i.

The heart–brain axis

Quantifying how cardiovascular dysfunction propagates to dementia, stroke, and cognitive decline — anchored by the KOVERI and AI4HOPE projects.

ii.

Translational medical imaging

Lightweight CNNs and hybrid models for MRI, CT, PET, and ultrasound. Brain tumour, stroke, and head-and-neck cancer pipelines suitable for near-point-of-care use.

iii.

Wearable physiological sensing

Multimodal biomedical signals — ECG, PPG, EEG, EMG, GSR — with a focus on minimal sensing and Polar SDK pipelines for real-time streaming.

iv.

Edge, IoT & federated learning

Edge–cloud workflows that minimise data transfer and respect privacy. Federated approaches deployed across the multi-site AI4HOPE consortium.

v.

Sleep, emotion & foundation models

Neonatal sleep stratification (>10% accuracy gain over benchmarks); emotion recognition with WESAD (+12% F1) and POPANE (+6%); PSG-trained foundation models.

Selected technical outputs

  • Neonatal sleep-staging pipeline outperforming prior benchmarks by >10%
  • Non-contact ECG monitoring for elderly care, foot/palm electrodes
  • WESAD emotion recognition: +12% macro-F1 vs. published baselines
  • POPANE emotion recognition: +6% over baselines
  • Lightweight MRI brain-tumour models suitable for point-of-care
  • Wearable edge–cloud workflows with Polar AccessLink SDK
  • Federated-learning setup across 17-partner EU consortium
