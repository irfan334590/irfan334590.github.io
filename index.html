Identity 03 / 12
Chapter II

Biomedical AI with one clear direction: from data to care.

"Efficient, explainable, privacy-aware — models that a clinician, a caregiver, and an engineer can all understand."

I am a Senior Researcher, Principal Investigator, Visiting Research Fellow and Supervisor working in artificial intelligence for healthcare. My expertise spans health monitoring, prevention, intervention, and treatment; wearable physiological sensing; edge AI and federated learning; and translational medical imaging.

I completed my first PhD in Biomedical Engineering at Fudan University in December 2024, and my second PhD in Information and Communication Engineering at the University of Turku in April 2026. Together they connect biomedical measurement, signal processing, imaging, and computationally efficient AI.

Focus

What I work on

Translating multimodal sleep, cardiovascular, physiological, and imaging biomarkers into deployable AI systems for dementia care, cardiovascular disease risk prediction, and personalised monitoring.

The bridge between hospital-grade signal quality and everyday wearable-grade data is where I concentrate my effort — that is where clinical translation actually happens.
